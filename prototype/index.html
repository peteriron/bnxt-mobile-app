iOS prototype · v0.1 · dark mode

BNXT League fan app

A clickable prototype for club supporters, laid out on a 402 × 874 pt iPhone screen. It runs on a real snapshot of public data from bnxtleague.com.

Jump to

Agreed spec

  • Who it's for: club supporters. You pick a club when you first open the app, and Home is built around that club.
  • Tabs: Home · Games · Standings · Stats · Clubs. Search is on Home and Clubs.
  • Live: score, clock and play-by-play, with a live box score. In this prototype the live game is simulated.
  • Game detail: quarter scores, team comparison, full box scores, top performers, arena, tickets and where to watch.
  • Standings: switch between the full table, the Belgian and Dutch tables, and a projected playoff bracket. Colours mark the Belgian (top 8) and Dutch (top 4) playoff places.
  • Stats: league leaders in 9 categories, team rankings, and side-by-side comparison of two players' season averages.
  • Later: push alerts, news and video, Dutch and French.
