Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026.

BNXT Basketball collects no personal data. There's no account, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your favourite club, so Home can show it.
  • A copy of the league data the app last downloaded (schedules, scores, standings, rosters), so it still works offline.

Deleting the app deletes both. Neither is sent to us or anyone else.

What the app downloads, and from where

To show league data the app requests it from these services. Like any internet request, they can see your IP address; the app sends them nothing about you.

  • The BNXT League's data service: scores, schedules, standings, stats, logos and photos.
  • FIBA LiveStats (Genius Sports): live play-by-play during games.
  • GitHub: the app's settings file (season details and club colours).

Links you choose to open, such as tickets, BNXT.tv or a game report, open in Safari or the website concerned, under its own policy. "Add to Calendar" uses the iPhone's own calendar screen; the app can't read your calendar.

Crash reports

If you've allowed it in iOS settings, Apple shares anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with developers. During the TestFlight beta, TestFlight also shares the feedback and screenshots you choose to send.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will say so, with a new date. Questions: info@pjproductions.be.
