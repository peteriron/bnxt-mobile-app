BNXT Basketball

Follow your club through the BNXT League, the joint top division of Belgium and the Netherlands: live scores and play-by-play, box scores, standings, the stat leaders and every club's roster and schedule.

Your club first. Pick your club once and Home shows its live or next game, last result, standing and leaders.

Every game. The full season schedule, results with box scores, and live games as they happen.

The whole league. Standings for the league and each country, stat leaders, and player and club pages.
