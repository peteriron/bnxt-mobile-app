Support

Something not working, a score that looks wrong, or an idea? Email info@pjproductions.be. Please mention your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (Settings in the app, under About).

Common questions

Where does the data come from? Scores, schedules, standings and stats come from the BNXT League. Live play-by-play comes from the league's live statistics provider, FIBA LiveStats.

A score or box score is behind. Live games update about every 10 seconds while the game screen is open. Right after the final whistle the official box score can take a few minutes to appear, and the league sometimes corrects it shortly after.

It says "Offline · updated …". The app couldn't reach the league just now and is showing the last data it saved. Pull down to refresh once you're back online.

How do I change my club? Home → the person icon (Settings) → My club → Change.
